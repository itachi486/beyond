# Current foundation update

BEYOND remains an account-free public curiosity universe, now with a private editorial admin and real SQLite persistence. ENTER THE UNKNOWN reaches the continuous six-destination `/explore` hub. Original four worlds and eight authored discoveries remain available. Six destinations are idempotently seeded without fabricated published discoveries; admin editing, source review and verification control the same public records.

The current implementation is stylized procedural Three.js, not a photorealistic universe. A real-time AI question engine, expanded authored discoveries, general scene editing and operator-grade hosting remain future scope. The latest integrated user brief supersedes historical Archive-first/no-backend statements below.

# BEYOND vision

**BEYOND — THE INTERNET WITHIN THE INTERNET** exists for wonder, curiosity and unexpected discovery.

“The existing internet helps people find what they want. BEYOND helps people discover what they didn't know they were looking for.”

Content is the world. A discovery is a destination, relationships are paths, and portals lead to other worlds. Scenery should invite exploration, while useful, sourced content makes it worth continuing.

The product is neither a conventional search engine nor a social feed, corporate dashboard, static marketing page or generic game. Search, if added, is secondary to serendipity. Fictional environments must remain distinguishable from factual knowledge.

## Settled landing policy

There is one primary entry action: **ENTER THE UNKNOWN**. No “Choose a world” UI or equivalent destination selector appears on the initial landing page, including menus or mobile alternatives. Entry requires a user action and reaches the existing first world, The Archive (`/worlds/archive`). Movement between worlds happens after entry through portals and their keyboard-accessible equivalents.

## Current foundation versus future vision

**Implemented foundation:** four stylized procedural worlds and eight locally bundled, sourced discoveries; EN/ES, personal browser-local collections, direct discovery URLs and accessible fallbacks.

**Future direction, not implemented:** a larger authored network, richer world-specific environments and more fully translated languages. No unlimited content, multiplayer, generative content service or photorealistic universe is claimed. Accounts, payments and a backend are not requirements for this local-first MVP.
